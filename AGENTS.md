## Non-negotiable current design intent
- Permanent annular mixed-program core + discrete outward removable 4.5 × 18 × 3.5 m modules (81 m²); central greenhouse/Infinite Rinse counted once. Base targets 7,500 / 8,000 / 1,500 / 1,000 / 2,000 m². Adjustable enclosed GFA cap default 20,000 m². Outdoor public realm excluded; enclosed roof retail and lift/service volumes included. Keep demand versus actual areas explicit; do not double-count common circulation.
- All distinct-program relationships default neutral, all clustering off. Click links to cycle attract/neutral/repel; drag nodes to change magnitude; click nodes to toggle optional clustering. Attraction should encourage adjacency and shared gathering lobbies, not guarantee them where geometry cannot fit. Repel means separate direct thresholds, not exclusion from shared public routes. Clustering strength applies to selected programs. Edge modes retain their weights at every clustering setting. Clustering is a soft alignment preference; do not permanently hard-code firehall clustering.
- Outer circulation reaches module doors; two opposed lifts, one aligned to swing access. Solid opaque exterior wall except intended openings; glass is primarily atrium roof. Slide endpoints connect directly to IR rooms; where slide blocks docks, suitable rooms take façade frontage and corridors bypass behind. Preserve shared lobbies visually as purple rugs/couches/gathering rooms. Minimum room area adjustable, default 80 m².
- Public annular roof (not glazed atrium) and continuous exterior promenade; connect entrance and climbing approach; preserve outdoor planting, activities and adaptable vacant docks. Neighborhood program influences amenity mix but retain variety.
- Prater-like swings through atrium: riders queue at boarding platform, load, ascend/accelerate, dwell at top 30 s, descend/decelerate and unload. Angle changes with speed; return to same boarding platform. Slide is medium blue-teal. Climb width adjustable, max height 19.8 m; always descend to base. Slide may cross the climbing strip by bowing outward; 5 m modeled clearance is an assumption, not certified safe distance. Reserve blocked docks before inventory placement.
- Human and non-human agents: diverse body sizes, wheelchair representation, indoor/public-network dogs, outdoor birds/rabbits/squirrels. Use generated walkable graph and elevators, not through rooms/walls; visitors dwell, enter/leave via plaza, remember last departed room and gravitate temporarily toward an attractive lobby; fun destinations matter. No claim of realistic observed behavior.
- Score actual eligible proximity encounters, not number of rooms/lobbies. Respect encounter context, credited duration, recent room/program and fun memory, different agent-kind bonus, visitor-time normalization. Do not change weights or comparison semantics invisibly. Keep live and independent five-minute trials distinct; compare like settings and multiple seeds.
