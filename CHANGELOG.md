# Changelog

## Seating pockets at bridge landings
- Fit three-seat capsules beside bridge approaches. Where space allows, up to five turn inward around a woven centre, with room for multiple independent groups. The route, door clearances and stairs stay open.
- Added a bridge seating toggle and optional simulation stops at the open side of each capsule.

## Program-led shared lobbies
- Removed standalone public lobbies from vacant bridge approaches and restored those bays to circulation, including in saved Materials studies. Shared lobbies still form where attracted programs can meet.

## Individual plug-in modules
- Added program similarity controls for seeded enclosures, claddings, openings, roof forms and short units with full-chassis terraces. Materials reports realized enclosed area and keeps the nominal massing allowances visible.
- Shared the generated openings and dock interface with Build. Added room partitions and program-specific furniture, separate interior and roof visibility, and varied garden, balcony and shop details.

## Full bridge lobbies
- Replaced side pockets with full two-sided public lobbies on feasible bridge approaches. Both arches align with the direct walk from the bridge to the outer corridor.
- Fitted shallow cabinet returns to the bay edges and routed simulated bridge arrivals through the furnished aisle.

## Public lobbies in open core bays
- Fitted furnished lobbies into large leftover core bays and gave bridge-side arrivals first choice of orientation. Each bay keeps a full-width radial lane and connections at both ends.
- Added the public lobbies to floor-area reporting, visitor routing, lighting and lobby drawings.

## Fitted lobby benches and paper lights
- Filled the clear gaps between the curved casework and programme walls with continuous, wall-scribed upholstered benches. The fit respects the planters and routes through each arch.
- Densified the overhead timber lattice while keeping the same support locations. Hung a paper ring pendant above the table, with coloured tabs inspired by the building's docking bays.

## Shared lobby locations and quarter domes
- Prioritized fitting a full shared lobby near a lift core or bridge approach, with a preference for connecting its corridor return directly to the junction. Removed the earlier freestanding landing furniture.
- Rebuilt the timber lattice as a quarter dome over one semicircular seating bay, with uprights that extend the generated casework and two intermediate supports.
- Increased maximum magnification fivefold in Massing and Build. Scroll and pinch now keep the detail under the cursor or pinch midpoint in view.

## Atrium stairs, landing lounges and lighting study

- Added Render after Build with independent daylight and interior-light controls, assembled materials, and level cutaways.
