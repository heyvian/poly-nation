Encounter breakdown
Scoring weights & method

Pairs within 2.5 m in the same accessible space (or adjoining corridor cells) earn points with time together. Each encounter earns at most 15 seconds of credit (base weight per 10 seconds); a pair must separate for 8 seconds before a new encounter. Memory records the last room or module departed, not the current one. Different remembered rooms, different programs, recent fun (90 seconds) and cross-species contact multiply the score. This is a design heuristic, not a measured social outcome.

Comparison history

Trials use the same seed, visitor count and scoring weights for fairer comparisons. Design changes restart live scoring. All active humans and animals contribute regardless of visibility. Live rates stabilize with time; use fixed-duration trials to compare designs.

How the area budget works

The selected cap includes the core, greenhouse, all enclosed pods and rooftop enclosures. Retail, entrance, corridors, lifts, shared lobbies, and enclosed roof structures count toward the cap. Circulation reduces the remaining core room allocations; installed pods retain their 81 m² area. Open balconies and gardens are tracked separately. Manual geometry may leave unused budget or program deficits. * Retail slots are not primary modules. Conceptual GFA, not code-verified.
