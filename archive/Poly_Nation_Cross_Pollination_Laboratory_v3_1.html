How the area budget works

The selected cap includes the core, greenhouse, all enclosed pods and rooftop enclosures. Retail, entrance, corridors, lifts, shared lobbies, and enclosed roof structures count toward the cap. Circulation reduces the remaining core room allocations; installed pods retain their 81 m² area. Open balconies and gardens are tracked separately. Manual geometry may leave unused budget or program deficits. * Retail slots are not primary modules. Conceptual GFA, not code-verified.
