# Authorship and collaboration

**Student-directed architectural design and research; AI-assisted computational development and iterative refinement.** This is a qualitative attribution, not a percentage of authorship or a university-policy determination. The student conceived the overarching architecture, program, relationships, and social research question and repeatedly steered revisions. ChatGPT produced the computational artifact and contributed algorithms, proposed numerical assumptions and implementation-level spatial/visual decisions. It would be inaccurate to say the AI only typed code or that the student personally authored every implemented rule. It would also be inaccurate to treat AI implementation as independent origination of the user's design concept.

## Evidence trail
`Design_Rationale.html` gives the detailed visual record and quotes from later user requests, including neutral defaults, clustering, lobby gathering, slide clearance, climbing descent, room memory, comparison intent and UI priorities. Earlier phases are reconstructed from the available project brief and conversation summaries rather than an exhaustive verbatim transcript. `COPILOT.md` records historical technical changes; it is implementation documentation, not proof of individual human authorship for every detail. Preserve these distinctions when updating presentations. Later user-directed changes should be attributed specifically, while computational methods and newly proposed defaults remain AI-assisted unless the student actually defines them.
