# Design history (reconstructed)

This is the available design trajectory, not a verbatim transcript. The illustrated record contains later direct quotations; earlier work is summarized from the brief and snapshots.

- Studio origins: Poly Nation / Infinite Rinse at former Canada Creosote site in West Village, Calgary. Architectural concept pairs remediation, public education and pleasure; five programs, adaptable permanent/module structure. The app is a concept-stage massing and encounter laboratory, not the treatment plant's engineering model.
- V1–V2.3: radial ring, adjustable geometry, module placement with topology, permanent/modular allocation, seeded variation, 20,000 m² cap, vacant slots become balconies/gardens/retail. AI implemented solver and accounting.
- V2.4–V2.6: variable entrance, central swing tower and exterior water slide, persistence, export, improved camera. Renderer and file interoperability developed iteratively.
- V2.7: student requests two opposed elevator blocks, adjustable corridors, direct attract links and shared lobbies, separate repel thresholds. AI adds annular access and common-space deduction.
- V2.8–V2.8.1: extended entrance plaza with public furniture; darker slide; all distinct-program relationships neutral initially, topology-only reset.
- V2.9–V2.9.3: direct IR slide rooms, bypass corridors, X-ray, public roof and pavilions, lift service volumes, cap slider, more zoom, swing animation; student corrects glass façade to opaque, adjusts slide toward teal, asks meaningful thickness; AI chooses 500 mm nominal.
- V2.9.4–V2.9.6: avoid wall/room z-fighting; shift eligible rooms to façade wherever slide reserves slots; cumulative floor cutaways; larger rooms (AI default 80 m²); people with varied bodies/wheelchairs and non-human placeholders.
- V3.0: AI UI overhaul, student later rejects excess changes. Visitor toggle bug repaired. Keeps grouped categories.
- V3.1–V3.2: student makes shared lobbies visibly furnished, introduces simulated behavior, room memory/temporary attraction, lifts, greenhouse and recreation; initially requests firehall clustering then generalizes per-program optional clustering, all off by default. Student proposes weighted encounters, last-room bonus, recent fun, cross-pollination as comparative output. AI implements graph, score math, trials, program/agent-kind modifiers.
- V3.3: width-controlled climbing wall; user corrects slide to bow outward instead of avoiding climb strip; max 19.8 m based on CCC reference; climbers always descend. AI chooses 5 m conceptual clearance and graph logic.
- V3.4–V3.5: student prioritizes score and topology; first dashboard above canvas, then user prefers original aesthetic, one small column, prominent topology/strength, taller canvas, score immediately BELOW model rather than a HUD.
- V3.6: student requests presentation-ready record of design authorship; full swing ride with queue/boarding/up/top dwell 30 s/down/unload and changing chain angles; wraparound plaza, connected entry and climbing, mixed outdoor amenities partially influenced by nearby modules. AI creates illustrated rationale and implementation. Baseline for Codex.

`archive/` retains available executable prior builds to inspect. `COPILOT.md` contains more granular release engineering history. These versions are not independent design alternatives or code-validated buildings.
